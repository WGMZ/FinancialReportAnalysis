## Conventions and period comparability

- **Periods**: `24Q3` to `26Q2` are actual calendar quarters; `26Q3E` is this report's forecast (purple / marked E). **YoY** compares with Q3'25, **QoQ** with Q2'26.

- **One-offs**: JPM's Q2'26 contains a one-off Visa gain of about $5.4B, shown separately or removed in QoQ comparisons and EPS trends (hatched in Figure 5, removed in Figures 6 and 7).

- **Q4 history**: XBRL does not report Q4 separately, so it is derived as FY minus 9M; EPS is therefore approximate.

- **Revenue basis**: total revenue = net interest income + non-interest income (GAAP, no FTE adjustment). The trading line is the income-statement trading revenue and excludes market-making NII, so it is lower than management's Markets revenue (e.g. JPM's Q2 Markets of $12.1B vs. $9.0B in the chart).

- **Colours**: blue = NII, orange = IB fees, green = trading, grey = other non-interest, purple = forecast; red = liability side or adverse, dark blue = asset side. Each bank keeps the same colour in every chart.

- **Units**: $bn unless noted; bp = basis point (0.01%); `$M` = millions of dollars.

- **Estimated vs. disclosed**: in the IPO tables, fees from prospectuses are marked 'disclosed', the rest 'est.'; per-bank splits are role-weighted estimates, not bank disclosures.

## Key findings at a glance

- **Curve**: the Treasury curve shifted up 32-90bp in Q3, with the 5Y up the most (+90bp); the 10Y reached 5.29% and 2s10s widened from 30bp to 41bp. The policy rate averaged only +4bp, +25bp at quarter-end. (Figures 1-3)

- **Repricing**: per $100bn of assets that reprice, the 5Y move adds about $0.90B a year of income; deposit costs at beta 40% add about $15M (Q3 average) or $100M (exit rate). Income arrives first; deposit cost catches up later. (Figure 4)

- **Revenue**: Q3E total revenue vs. Q2'26 (JPM ex-Visa): JPM -1.1%, BAC -4.8%, C -2.4%, WFC -1.1%, GS -13.8%, MS -3.0%; YoY all are positive (+4.4% to +15.5%). The QoQ decline comes mainly from trading and IB. (Figures 5-6)

- **AFS/HTM**: BAC's HTM unrealised loss is $82.1B, WFC's $32.4B, JPM's $18.2B; these do not enter regulatory capital but are a hidden loss of economic book value. (Figures 10-12)

- **Stress test**: a further +100bp parallel hike, including the HTM economic loss, gives JPM -$14.0B (-4.7% of TCE), BAC -$21.3B (-10.3% of TCE), C -$7.9B (-4.7% of TCE), WFC -$11.4B (-8.1% of TCE), GS -$4.6B (-4.6% of TCE), MS -$3.5B (-4.1% of TCE). BAC is the most sensitive, almost entirely through HTM. (Figures 13-14)

- **IPO**: we reviewed 18 Q3 deals raising about $32.6B, with estimated underwriting fees of about $611M in total (SK hynix about $258M); the six large banks together earn about $376M, equal to 0.5%-6.9% of each bank's Q3E IB fees. The big Q4 variable is Anthropic (media reports; no public S-1). (Figure 15)

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| NII / NIM | Net interest income / net interest margin |
| AFS / HTM | Available-for-sale securities (fair value through AOCI) / held-to-maturity securities (amortised cost; unrealised losses do not enter capital) |
| AOCI | Accumulated other comprehensive income; cumulative AFS gains and losses |
| TCE / TBV | Tangible common equity / tangible book value = equity - goodwill - intangibles - preferred |
| IB / S&T | Investment banking / sales and trading |
| 2s10s | 10-year minus 2-year Treasury yield |
| Bear steepener / flattener | Rates rise while the curve steepens / flattens |
| Deposit beta | Change in deposit rates ÷ change in the policy rate |
| Duration | Approximate % price change for a 1-point move in rates |
| P/E (TTM) | Price ÷ trailing-12-month EPS |
